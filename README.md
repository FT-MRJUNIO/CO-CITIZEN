Co-Citizen
Collective Voices. Collective Action.

Co-Citizen is a citizen-powered platform that enables people and government to work together through transparency, accountability, and community-driven solutions.

I'm not just a citizen. I'm a Co-Citizen.

I participate.
 I report issues.
 I help monitor my community.
 I contribute to transparency.
 I help improve governance.

Core Principle

Every module follows the same cycle:

Issue → Discussion → Verification → Solution → Tracking → Public Accountability

Not just reporting problems.

Finding solutions together.

📋 Permit Auditing
Issue & Solution

Transparency in government permits and processes.

Are requirements clear?
Are timelines reasonable?

Mission Statement

Co-Citizen empowers people to collectively identify problems, verify facts, propose solutions, and drive meaningful action in their communities. Through transparency, accountability, and participation, every citizen becomes a partner in governance.

That's a mission strong enough for a movement, not just an application.
